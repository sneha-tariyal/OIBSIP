# EDA on Retail Sales Data

## Project Overview
This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on retail sales data to identify sales trends, customer behaviour patterns, product performance, and actionable business insights.

## Objective
The objective of this project is to analyse retail sales data using Python and uncover useful patterns and insights related to sales, customers, products, and payment methods.

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Data Inspection
- Dataset shape
- Column data types
- Missing value check
- Duplicate value check

### 2. Descriptive Statistics
Calculated:
- Mean
- Median
- Mode
- Standard Deviation

for numerical columns.

### 3. Time Series Analysis
Analysed:
- Monthly sales trends
- Quarterly sales trends

using line charts.

### 4. Customer Demographics
Analysed:
- Customer age groups
- Gender distribution

### 5. Product Analysis
Analysed:
- Top 10 best-selling products based on quantity sold
- Revenue by product category

### 6. Correlation Analysis
Created a correlation heatmap to understand relationships between numerical variables such as:
- Age
- Price Per Unit
- Quantity
- Total Spent

### 7. Additional Analysis
Analysed average spending by payment method to identify differences in customer spending behaviour.

## Key Insights
- Butchers was the highest-revenue product category.
- Item_2_BEV had the highest quantity among the top 10 best-selling products.
- Quantity and Total Spent showed a strong positive correlation of approximately 0.71.
- Payment methods were compared based on average customer spending.

## Business Recommendations
1. Prioritize high-performing product categories by maintaining sufficient inventory and using targeted promotions.
2. Focus on high-demand products to avoid stock shortages and support consistent sales.
3. Encourage higher-value purchases through bundles and multi-unit offers, as higher quantities are associated with higher total spending.
